How it is calculated

  • Battery rent + utilities is charged every month in every scenario.
  • IP rent and the assessed care fee are prorated by days at IP in each month.
  • Home care is priced per hour, day by day. At Battery from Nov 1: round-the-clock care for the first 72 hours (3 days), then 8 hours a day. After moving back from IP there is no round-the-clock period; the daily hours are an estimate (8 to start with). At IP: three 4-hour sessions a week for the whole stay (12 hours a week, spread evenly across the days).
  • Visiting family: one week a month, family uses her apartment when she is at IP at no cost. In months she is at Battery they stay in a hotel, so the hotel cost counts against the at-home scenarios (prorated by days at home in a mixed month).
  • The community fee is paid on Nov 1. The stay is counted in days from Nov 1 to the last day of rent, so leaving Dec 1 is 30 days (80%), Jan 1 is 61 days (40%) and Feb 1 is 92 days (no refund). If you leave within 30 days the refund is 80% of the fee, within 60 days 60%, within 90 days 40%, after that nothing. The assessment deduction comes off the refund amount (for example 80% of the fee, minus the deduction).
  • The IP assessment is finished, so the 100% refund for deciding not to move before it completes no longer applies. Not moving in at all gets the 80% tier (30-day notice), less the assessment deduction, so the first scenario still pays 20% of the fee plus that deduction.
  • "Stay" assumes you are still living at IP at the end of February, so no refund is received in this window.
